About me

Hello! I'm a software engineer who enjoys building tools that simplify, optimize and enhance the way people work. I started at Disneyland Resort learning time and attendance systems and writing SQL audits, got picked for a project hire to build my first production app, and today I build and run HR technology for Global HR Operations at The Walt Disney Company.

I have experience coding from scratch, building with AI-assisted development, and blending the two, always guided by clear specs and careful review. I've also trained colleagues on tools like Claude and Copilot. I enjoy partnering with the people who use my tools, figuring out systems nobody has documented, and leaving things better than I found them.
